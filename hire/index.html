What I do

Three kinds of work, because those are the three I can prove. Anything outside them I will tell you is outside them.

1 · Technical & programmatic SEO

For sites that are already built and are not being found.

  • A crawl and index audit that names the pages Google is skipping and why, rather than a score out of a hundred.
  • Programmatic pages generated at build time — thousands of them, statically exported, each one worth indexing rather than padding a sitemap.
  • Core Web Vitals fixed at the cause, and Cloudflare configured at the edge: caching, redirects, and WAF rules that do not block the crawler by accident.
Check it yourself HARvest — a 28-rule HTTP audit Cloudflare WAF rules Login Hub PK — programmatic, static

Typical engagement: audit from $900; a programmatic build from $3,500, scoped and quoted before anything starts.

2 · Product build — Flutter and Node, end to end

For a product that has to exist on a phone and in a browser, with one backend behind both.

  • Flutter for Android and iOS from one codebase, including the awkward part most quotes leave out: the bridge down to native when the platform will not do it in Dart.
  • A Node API and a real schema behind it, deployed — Docker, Nginx, Cloudflare, signing keys held outside the app repository.
  • Store submission, and the release plumbing nobody enjoys: signing, staged rollout, crash reporting, and the second submission after the first is rejected.
Check it yourself Saku — a service worker answering Android's share sheet Cek Aman — case study BioAge — case study

Typical engagement: a first shippable version from $6,000, or $45–75/hour on an existing codebase.

3 · Data correctness and legacy rescue

For the database that is quietly wrong, and the PHP nobody left will touch.

  • Schema migrations against rows that already exist, including the rebuild that commits, passes every integrity check, reports no error — and has silently deleted rows.
  • Slow queries read through the real plan rather than guessed at, and the indexes that follow from it.
  • Legacy PHP brought forward — the mysql_* era, applications on an unmaintained framework, systems whose author has left.
One thing I have that an offshore quote usually does not. Indonesian regulatory domain, implemented and asserted rather than read about: PPh 21 under the TER regime, BPJS, FIFO costing with backdated recalculation, a cash flow statement that reconciles to the ledger, medical record numbering, PPDB admissions. If your product has to be correct under Indonesian rules, that is usually the expensive part, not the CRUD around it.
Check it yourself Rombak — 262 properties on migrations Payroll — PPh 21 TER Buku — a reconciling cash flow statement

Typical engagement: $50–85/hour, or a fixed price once I have seen the schema. A one-week rescue is usually enough to know which it should be.

What I will not take on

Every lab on this site ships its limits as part of the lab. The same applies to me.

I am not a designer. I can build a clean, accessible interface from a design, or from nothing when the product is mostly mechanism. I cannot give you brand work, and a project whose success is mostly visual identity should go to someone whose portfolio is visual.
Native iOS and Android are a bridge, not a speciality. I write the platform channel when Flutter cannot reach something — an Android notification listener, a share target, signed release plumbing. A pure Swift or Kotlin codebase is not my strength and I would rather tell you that now than three weeks in.
I integrate AI models, I do not train them. Vision and language models in shipped products, with the cost, latency and failure handling that takes. Not model training, not research, and not a claim to accuracy I have not measured.
US working hours do not fit. Colombo is GMT+5:30. Europe, the Gulf and Southeast Asia overlap my day comfortably; US Pacific does not, and a role that needs four hours of daily overlap with California is one I would be bad at rather than one I would stretch to.
SEO that games rather than earns. No link networks, no cloaking, no doorway pages. Programmatic pages have to be worth indexing on their own, and if the honest version of a page has nothing to say, the answer is not to generate it ten thousand times.
